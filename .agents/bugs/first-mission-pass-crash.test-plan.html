In-Water Pass

  1. Start mission 1 and keep the player alive.
  2. Move the Admiral into water until the mission pass sequence begins.
  3. Observe the pass message, fade, cleanup, next marker, and control return.

Expected: The pass sequence completes without an unhandled exception, and the player returns to free roam with the next mission marker available.

Car-Dead Pass With Player Alive

  1. Start mission 1 and keep the player outside the Admiral and alive.
  2. Destroy the Admiral without killing or busting the player.
  3. Observe the pass sequence and the transition to free roam.

Expected: The Car.IsDead($Admiral) branch registers the mission pass, cleans up the mission, and returns control without a crash.

Death And Arrest Control

  1. Start mission 1.
  2. Cause the player to be wasted or busted before the Admiral pass condition.
  3. Observe the mission failure path and hospital or police respawn.

Expected: The mission follows its death/arrest failure handler. Do not count this as a successful car-dead pass.

Evidence And Pass Criteria

  • Record which pass condition occurred and whether the player was alive and in a vehicle.
  • If the game crashes, capture the full exception report and the final Intro trace entries, including the last opcode before the trace stops.
  • Record the workspace and installed SCM hashes for the tested run.
  • Record whether the pass text, cleanup, fade-in, next marker, and player control completed.
