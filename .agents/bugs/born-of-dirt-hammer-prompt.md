# Born of Dirt Hammer Prompt

This document records the symptom and expected behavior from the playtest report.

## Symptom

In “Born of Dirt,” picking up the Hammer is expected to prompt the player to step up to the counter. Instead, the player gets a wanted level and is told to lose the cops.

## Scope

- The Hammer pickup and subsequent counter/wanted-level behavior occur in the `Hotel` mission, at `main.txt` lines 9302 to 9333 (`:Hotel_1361`, `:Hotel_1450`, and `:Hotel_1457`).
