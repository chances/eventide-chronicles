    How the tracker works

    • MAIN starts the PackageToggle thread. The tracker itself starts off.
    • P turns it on and starts the PACKAGE thread. O turns it off and ends that thread.
    • A red blip marks the current package. Coming within 3.5 units moves it to the next location.
    • O then P starts again at the first location.
    • After a death or bust, both threads wait and resume instead of running into Get_Package_Coords.

      Not covered: the reward pickups at 10, 20, 45, 60, and 80 packages. Reaching them takes too long for a quick test.
