Ringtone Editor

Click an empty cell to add a note. Drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to resize it, and right-click or press Delete to remove it. Space plays and stops.

Templates

Piano Roll

WAV export is mono, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz. The game plays it from the Audio folder with the script audio stream command.

Reference Tones

US ringback is 440 Hz plus 480 Hz, 2 seconds on and 4 seconds off. US busy is 480 Hz plus 620 Hz, 0.5 seconds on and 0.5 seconds off.
