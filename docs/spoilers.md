# Spoilers

This document records story facts that the player must not learn from the mod's text, art, or store listings. Contributors read it to make art, script, and writing decisions. Do not repeat these facts in [branding.md](branding.md), [lore.md](lore.md), [visual-identity.md](visual-identity.md), release art, or any player-facing string.

Status: Draft. The ledger rows that point here are in [lore.csv](lore.csv).

## The Hidden Premise

The protagonist, Toni Mueller, is in a coma. The world the player explores is a hallucination, or a half-remembered dream. The player is not told.

Every unreliable account in the mod has a discoverable truth. This is the largest one. The opening narrator's claim that the player cannot die inside the Barrier is false in the way a dream's rules are false. [Unreliable Narrator](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/UnreliableNarrator) applies to the whole game.

## The Barrier

The Barrier is physical in the world. Starfish Island is locked at the start. The obstacles stand for Toni's psyche and her mental blocks. They are not a literal wall around the city.

## Mood and Coherence

Mood is Toni's emotional state. Coherence is how far her reality has broken. Both are defined in [visual-identity.md](visual-identity.md). Coherence rises as the story proceeds, so her perception fails first in small ways and then in large ones.

## Leaks

A leak is a detail from outside the dream. Leaks start rare and unmistakable, and they grow more frequent and more explicit across the story. They are defined by the stage table in [visual-identity-coherence.csv](visual-identity-coherence.csv).

The research below guides what a leak can be. Use dream logic, not clinical claims.

- ICU survivors report vivid, often frightening dream memories. Many cannot tell dream, delirium, and real events apart ([PMC study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8046113)).
- Survivors of induced coma most often recall thirst, cold, and pain ([Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem review](https://doaj.org/article/2695b4dcd40a4d388e3bb93e7075d68d)).
- Real ward sounds can pass into a dream. A first-person case is summarized in [Mind Hacks](https://mindhacks.com/2014/04/13/coma-alarm-dreams/).
- Whether trauma can block memory is contested ([Otgaar et al., 2019](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/22336/)). The mod therefore does not present repression as a medical fact.

## Rules for Public Material

- Release art, loading screens, and listings do not state or hint at the premise.
- A leak that appears in a screenshot must have a deniable surface reading.
- Contributors who write about a leak in a commit message or issue link to this document and do not describe it.
